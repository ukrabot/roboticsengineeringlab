Build and Control a 6-DOF Robot Arm

Six practical tutorials that anchor this site — design and wire a robot arm with Arduino, control it with ROS 2, and integrate it into an industrial PLC workflow.

How to read this site: Robotics Engineering Lab is an independent editorial project about 6-DOF robot arms. Arduino, wiring and kinematics pages include worked examples and code you can reproduce. Pages about FANUC, ABB, KUKA, Universal Robots and similar industrial hardware are specification-based buying notes, not lab tests — we do not have those machines on a bench, and those articles say so at the top.

Numbers come from manufacturer documentation, standards bodies (ANSI, CSA, ISO) or a calculation shown on the page. If a figure is wrong or outdated, send a correction.
