How to read this site: Robotics Engineering Lab is an independent editorial project about robot arms and classroom robotics. The Arduino, sensor, wiring and kinematics guides are built and tested in the classroom, with worked examples, real photos and code you can reproduce. A few pages cover industrial concepts (safety standards, PLCs, calibration) from official documentation rather than a bench test, and each one says so at the top.

Numbers come from manufacturer documentation, standards bodies (ANSI, CSA, ISO) or a calculation shown on the page. If a figure is wrong or outdated, send a correction.
