Build and Control a 6-DOF Robot Arm

The three core tutorials that anchor this site — design and wire a robot arm with Arduino, control it with ROS 2, and integrate it into an industrial PLC workflow.

About this site: Robotics Engineering Blog is a small, independent editorial project focused on 6-DOF robot arms — Arduino and ROS 2 control, mechanical design, and industrial automation. Tutorials that involve physical builds (Arduino wiring, 3D-printed parts, gripper design) are based on hands-on prototyping. Manufacturer hardware reviews (FANUC, ABB, KUKA, etc.) are clearly labeled as specification-based editorial comparisons, since we do not have physical access to $30,000+ industrial robot arms for independent lab testing — we say so explicitly in each of those articles rather than implying hands-on testing that didn't happen.

We link directly to primary sources — manufacturer datasheets, open-source repositories, and safety standards bodies (ANSI, CSA, ISO) — wherever a specific number or claim is made. If you spot an error or an outdated figure, please let us know.
